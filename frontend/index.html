The project

From diesel pits to solar sites

Traditional mine dewatering uses diesel pumps that run continuously, wasting fuel and damaging ecosystems. Our solar-powered, sensor-driven system activates only when moisture exceeds thresholds — slashing emissions and operational costs.

Traditional issues:
  • Diesel combustion → CO₂ & particulate matter
  • Constant pumping → groundwater depletion
  • Remote fuel logistics → high OPEX
